# Changelog

All notable changes to Canvas are listed here. Canvas follows [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).

## 1.20.1 (2026-11-22)

- The sort menu on listing and search pages shows the sorts the store offers, in its order and under its names, in the shopper's language. The theme's own sort names are gone.

## 1.20.0 (2026-11-17)

First public release. Canvas's version numbers continue from its Theme Store listing.

- 70 sections covering every storefront page: home-page sections, product, listings, search, cart and cart drawer, account, sign-in, blog, gift cards, wishlist, the gate pages (coming soon, password, maintenance) and the overlays (cookie banner, age gate, country prompt, popups).
- 20 theme blocks for section content and footer columns.
- Three header layouts and three footer layouts.
- Three style presets: Everyday, Editorial and Night.
- Storefront text in ten languages (en, de, es, fr, it, ar, he, hi, th, zh-TW) and editor labels in five (en, de, es, fr, it).
- A demo catalog for local previews.
