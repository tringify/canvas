{%- comment -%} Gift card recipient, inside a cart_add form for a gift card. Input: cv_gid (unique id prefix). The section needs forms in ctx_needs: every maxlength and the message counter come from forms.cart_add. Off: the card goes to the buyer's checkout contact. On: the fields open and post with Add to cart and Buy now; while off they are disabled, so nothing is sent. Send on: without script, the native date-time field posts gift_card_deliver_at itself (the platform reads a bare value as UTC, so its hint says so). With script, the picker in the template replaces it: Now (after payment), Tomorrow morning or a day and time, in the shopper's own time zone, posted as RFC 3339 with that moment's offset. The platform only requires a future moment, so the calendar has no last day. Channels: the email field shows only while forms.cart_add.gift_card_recipient_email.available, the phone field only while gift_card_recipient_phone.available, and the hint and the "add a contact" message name only what is shown. With neither, the store cannot send a card to anyone else, so the whole switch is left out and the card goes to the buyer. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cv_g_email = forms.cart_add.gift_card_recipient_email.available -%}{%- assign cv_g_phone = forms.cart_add.gift_card_recipient_phone.available -%} {%- if cv_g_email or cv_g_phone -%} {%- if cv_g_email and cv_g_phone -%}{%- assign cv_g_reach = 'products.gift_reach_hint' | t -%}{%- assign cv_g_need = 'products.gift_need_contact' | t -%} {%- elsif cv_g_email -%}{%- assign cv_g_reach = 'products.gift_reach_email' | t -%}{%- assign cv_g_need = 'products.gift_need_email' | t -%} {%- else -%}{%- assign cv_g_reach = 'products.gift_reach_phone' | t -%}{%- assign cv_g_need = 'products.gift_need_phone' | t -%}{%- endif -%}
