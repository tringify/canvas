Reads cart, design_mode and the block (b); cv_where keeps the page's and the drawer's ids apart. Each fee is a checkbox in its own cart_fee_select form, ticked when the cart has chosen it; partials/optional-services-script sends a change at once and puts the box back when the platform refuses it (the platform shows its message). Checkout prices and taxes what is chosen. A fixed fee prints the platform's amount (per item when it is charged per item), a percent fee its share of the subtotal (and its cap, percent_cap). Without script each fee has an Add or Remove button instead of the box. A fee the cart does not meet (applies false) shows the platform's unavailable_reason; its box is disabled unless the cart chose it, which then reads Not applied and can still be removed.
