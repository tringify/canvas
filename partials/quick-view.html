{%- comment -%} Quick view (theme setting "Choose options opens": Quick view). Any link marked data-cv-qv (a card's Choose options, Shop the look, the hero's pins, Featured product) opens its product over the page: a side drawer on wider screens, a bottom sheet on phones. The content is the product page's own buy box, rendered by the platform for this product and this shopper (?_sections=main-product on the product's address, so the language, market and prices are the page's): media, title, price, options, quantity, Add to cart and Buy it now. Picking an option renders it again for the new choice. Add to cart posts through the platform like on the product page; after an add the drawer closes and the cart behaves as "After adding to cart" says. When the product cannot be shown (an alternative product template without the Product section, a network error) the link opens the product page instead. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if theme.settings.card_choose_opens != 'product_page' -%}
